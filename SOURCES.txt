# Sources and data preparation

Prepared 12 October 2026. These are published model-show records, not invented
observations. These competitions are examples, not a national hobby survey.

## Primary event sources

- ACT Scale Modellers' Society: [ScaleACT 2025 results](https://actsms.enter.hobbyshow.org/show-results/10?view=results).
  Use the **Class awards** section only: 817 entries, including No Award and
  Junior Achievement. Special awards repeat some entries and are not added.
- [ScaleACT 2025 organiser's report](https://www.actsms.asn.au/about/previous-comps-results/scaleact-25-comp-results)
  provides another check on the event total.
- Illawarra Plastic Modellers' Association: [NSW 2025 statistics](https://ipma.enter.hobbyshow.org/show-results/5?view=stats),
  with 313 entries, and [NSW 2026 statistics](https://ipma.enter.hobbyshow.org/show-results/6?view=stats),
  with 322 entries. Category and scale statistics are available on these pages.

The underlying published statistics endpoints used for comparison were:

- https://actsms.enter.hobbyshow.org/api/show-stats/10/categories
- https://actsms.enter.hobbyshow.org/api/show-stats/10/scales
- https://ipma.enter.hobbyshow.org/api/show-stats/5/categories
- https://ipma.enter.hobbyshow.org/api/show-stats/5/scales
- https://ipma.enter.hobbyshow.org/api/show-stats/6/categories
- https://ipma.enter.hobbyshow.org/api/show-stats/6/scales

Model entries are different from competitors or registrations. A person may
enter several models. No chart here treats the number of models as people.

## What each data file contains

| File | Preparation and denominator |
|---|---|
| `data_heatmap.json` | Count ScaleACT entries by broad category and scale. Include zero-count combinations. Combine scales outside the nine named choices into Other scales. `within_category = entries / all entries in that category`. Total: 817. |
| `data_countries.json` | Match single-brand manufacturer labels to the 19 profiles below, then total by company country. `aircraft_share = aircraft_entries / entries`; `mapped_share = entries / 353`. Total: 353. |
| `data_brands.json` | Count matched entries by category, brand and country. The chart filters first, then adds the category rows for each brand. Total: 353. |
| `data_country_mix.json` | Group matched entries into Japan, Europe, China, and US & South Korea, separately for each category. `within_category = entries / matched entries in that category`. Total: 353. |
| `data_scales.json` | Counts for seven selected scales from ScaleACT 2025 and NSW 2026. Divide by 817 or 322 respectively. These seven scales do not exhaust either event. |
| `data_trend.json` | Published NSW event totals: 313 in 2025 and 322 in 2026. Increase = 9 / 313, or 2.9%. Only year and entries are drawn. |
| `world-map.json` | Natural Earth country geometry distributed by [Vega datasets](https://github.com/vega/vega-datasets/blob/main/data/world-110m.json). Public-domain Natural Earth boundaries; this is supplied geometry. |

Country coordinates are approximate reference locations used to place symbols
and relationship endpoints. They are not addresses, factory sites, shipment
origins or builders' residences. Canberra is represented by 149.13°E, 35.28°S.

## Category grouping

Class-number prefixes and skill-level suffixes are not plotted. Related class
labels are grouped as follows. Junior and named theme classes take precedence
over subject words in those labels.

| Group | Source labels included |
|---|---|
| Aircraft | Aircraft and helicopters. |
| Military vehicles | Military vehicles and AFVs. |
| Civilian vehicles | Civilian vehicles, motorsport, motorcycles and trucks. |
| Ships | Ships, submarines and maritime subjects. |
| Sci-fi & mecha | Science fiction, Gundam/mecha, Maschinen, spacecraft, Star Wars, gaming, egg models and fantasy. |
| Figures | Figures and busts. |
| Dioramas / Vignettes | Their respective scene classes, kept separate. |
| Junior | Classes labelled junior. |
| Special themes | Named theme classes such as Kittyhawk Cup, Sentinel Shield, The Grail and Commonwealth Cup, within Class awards. |
| Open / other | Open and scratchbuilt classes. |

## Manufacturer matching and limits

Manufacturer labels are normalised for case, spaces and punctuation, then
compared with exact aliases for the brands below. For example, GW maps to Games
Workshop, and the recorded spelling Trumpter maps to Trumpeter. Compound,
custom, unknown and unverified labels remain unmatched. This deliberately
conservative subset contains 353 entries, 43.2% of 817; 464 remain unmatched.

The company-country maps and brand charts therefore do not describe the whole
competition or global market share. The heatmap and scale totals still include
all entries in their event. The choropleth needs at least ten matched entries
per country; Ukraine (7) and Poland (5) remain grey.

Europe combines UK, Czechia, Germany, Italy, Ukraine and Poland. The US & South
Korea group combines those two countries. These groups reflect this matched
sample, not a complete classification of manufacturers worldwide.
