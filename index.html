THE WORKBENCH

Aircraft are the largest category

Builders assemble and paint miniature aircraft, vehicles, ships and imagined worlds. At Canberra’s ScaleACT 2025, aircraft made up almost a third of the 817 entries. Each entry is a model, and one person can enter several.

AIRCRAFT ENTRIES 31.7%

259 aircraft entries make this the largest category. Civilian vehicles (116) and military vehicles (104) follow.

Dioramas and vignettes are miniature scenes. Mecha are fictional robots. Junior and special-theme entries retain the organiser’s class groupings.

ENTRIES AND REGISTRATIONS

More entries, fewer registrations

At the NSW show, model entries increased between 2025 and 2026 while registrations declined. Looking at both measures gives a fuller picture than the entry total alone.

The recorded totals
Measure 2025 2026
Model entries 313 322
Registrations 95 83
Entries per registration 3.29 3.88

The ratio rose by 17.7%. It is an event-level average: it does not mean every participant entered more models.

Two observations at one show do not establish a national or long-term trend. They do show why models and registrations should be counted separately.

Aircraft led the ScaleACT categories, but popular scales differed between shows. In NSW, more model entries did not mean more registrations.
